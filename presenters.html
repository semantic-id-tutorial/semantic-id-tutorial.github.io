Presenters

The tutorial is presented by seven researchers from Huawei Ireland Research Center and University College Dublin.

Roger Zhe Li

Huawei Ireland Research Center, Dublin, Ireland

Senior Research Scientist at the Huawei Ireland Research Center, leading ads recommendation R&D covering multimodal semantic IDs and CVR prediction, with the system serving over 250 million users globally. He holds a PhD from TU Delft, with publications at SIGIR, WSDM, RecSys, and ICTIR. He was recognized as an outstanding PC member at RecSys '25, and served as an invited industry speaker on multimodal semantic IDs at ECIR '26.

Homepage · Email

Aghiles Salah

Huawei Ireland Research Center, Dublin, Ireland

Principal Research Scientist at the Huawei Ireland Research Center. His research spans machine learning and recommender systems, with publications in tier-1 conferences and journals. He co-delivered hands-on tutorials on multimodal recommender systems at RecSys '21 and WebConf '23, and created and is a main contributor to the Cornac recommendation framework. For more than a decade, he taught undergraduate and master's courses on machine learning and recommender systems at the University of Paris, France.

Homepage · Email

Mete Sertkan

Huawei Ireland Research Center, Dublin, Ireland

Senior Research Scientist at the Huawei Ireland Research Center working on ads recommendation. His research interests span recommender systems, user modeling, information retrieval, and evaluation, with publications in top-tier venues including ACL, CIKM, UMAP, RecSys, and SIGIR. He holds a PhD from TU Wien, where he taught master's courses on recommender systems, social network analysis, and innovation.

Homepage · Email

Changhong Jin

University College Dublin, Dublin, Ireland

Assistant Professor of Computer Science at University College Dublin. His recent work covers recommender-system auditing, ad recommendation under limited availability of personalized features, and generative recommendation with semantic IDs. He has published at CIKM, The Web Conference, and ACL, and delivered a one-hour tutorial on recommender-system auditing at the ECML-PKDD 2025 AIDEM workshop.

Email

Yingjie Niu

Huawei Ireland Research Center, Dublin, Ireland

Research Scientist at the Huawei Ireland Research Center, working on ads recommendation and multimodal semantic IDs. His research interests span recommender systems and large language models, with publications in tier-1 venues including ACL and WWW. He holds a PhD from University College Dublin, where he taught master's courses on machine learning and information systems.

Ruihai Dong

University College Dublin, Dublin, Ireland

Associate Professor of Computer Science at University College Dublin. His research focuses on machine learning and deep learning with applications in recommender systems and finance. He has published widely in top venues (e.g., WWW, RecSys, ACL, IJCAI) and served on their program committees. He co-authored the RecSys '23 Best Student Paper and has co-organized the EARL Workshop series on Evaluating and Applying Recommender Systems with Large Language Models, co-located with RecSys since 2024.
